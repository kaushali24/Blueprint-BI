## Context

The `business-analytics-foundation` change introduces a read-only analytics layer. This layer sits above the database and below the future AI business assistant or frontend API. It uses deterministic SQLAlchemy queries to calculate metrics from the structured data generated by the `ai-business-extraction` pipeline.

The target models are `Order`, `OrderItem`, `Inquiry`, `Customer`, and `Feedback`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Provide deterministic, mathematically accurate aggregations.
- Enforce strict business isolation in every query.
- Explicitly handle missing monetary values (NULLs) to avoid misleading totals.
- Deliver typed response DTOs for structured consumption.

**Non-Goals:**
- No LLM orchestration, RAG, or vector databases.
- No new extraction logic or NLP based sentiment modeling.
- No direct reading or interpretation of raw WhatsApp `Message` content.
- No customer identity merging/resolution (relies strictly on existing `customer_id` associations).
- No frontend dashboard implementation.
- No predictive ML or advanced forecasting.

## Decisions

**Decision 1: SQLAlchemy Query Boundary**
All metrics will be calculated using SQLAlchemy Core/ORM queries executed directly against the database. The `AnalyticsService` will strictly read from the `Order`, `OrderItem`, `Inquiry`, `Customer`, and `Feedback` models. 
*Rationale:* Ensures mathematical accuracy, high performance, and relies on the established schema.

**Decision 2: Strict Business Isolation**
Every query executed by the `AnalyticsService` must include a `WHERE business_id = :business_id` clause.
*Rationale:* Fundamental tenant of a multi-tenant system to prevent cross-business data leakage.

**Decision 3: Monetary NULL Semantics**
In the extraction layer, `unit_price`, `line_total`, and `total_amount` can be `NULL` if the price was unknown. 
When aggregating totals (e.g., total sales), a standard `SUM` operation ignores `NULL`s. To prevent presenting a misleading total, the service must report both the `known_total_revenue` AND a count of `orders_with_unknown_revenue_count`. 
*Rationale:* An LLM reading `total sales: $50` might assume that represents the entirety of the business's revenue. `known_total_revenue` represents ONLY the known monetary value of confirmed Orders and may be incomplete when `orders_with_unknown_revenue_count > 0`. It is NOT guaranteed complete business revenue. `NULL` MUST NOT be converted to zero. Do NOT derive total revenue by guessing `unit_price * quantity` unless the approved extraction/data model explicitly establishes that rule (which it doesn't).

**Decision 4: Status Filtering and Semantics**
Metrics must respect the entity statuses defined by the schema and extraction rules:
- **Orders:** Grouped and counted by `status` ('confirmed', 'pending', 'cancelled', 'inquiry'). Only 'confirmed' orders are included in revenue/sales totals.
- **Inquiries:** Grouped and counted by `status` (default 'open').
- **Feedback:** Grouped by `sentiment`.
*Rationale:* Prevents uncertain or unconfirmed data from inflating core business metrics. 

**Decision 5: Customer and Repeat Customer Logic**
A repeat customer is an existing Customer associated through `customer_id` with MORE THAN ONE CONFIRMED Order belonging to the same business. 
Rules:
- confirmed orders count;
- pending orders do NOT count;
- cancelled orders do NOT count;
- inquiry-status Orders do NOT count;
- NULL `customer_id` does NOT count;
- analytics MUST NOT perform identity resolution.

**Decision 6: Product Aggregation Semantics**
Group by the persisted `product_name` value. Only `OrderItems` belonging to confirmed Orders count. Business isolation must be enforced through the parent Order (`OrderItem -> Order -> business_id` since `OrderItem` has no `business_id`). `SUM(quantity)` determines ordered quantity. `COUNT(OrderItem.id)` may represent order-line frequency. Deterministic ranking for product ties MUST be used: `total_quantity DESC`, `line_count DESC`, `product_name ASC`. Do NOT introduce fuzzy product matching, plural normalization, embeddings, LLMs, or product identity resolution in analytics.

**Decision 7: Response DTOs and Recent Entities**
The service will return lightweight Pydantic models (e.g., `OrderMetricsDTO`, `CustomerMetricsDTO`). We also define `RecentOrderDTO` and `RecentInquiryDTO` containing only fields needed by downstream consumers (do NOT expose SQLAlchemy ORM objects directly). The service will expose a composite `BusinessAnalyticsReportDTO` combining all individual metrics, which matches the future AI-assistant consumption boundary. Deterministic ordering for recent lists: `created_at DESC`, `id DESC` as a tie-breaker. Since `created_at` is `nullable=False`, NULL ordering behavior is not needed.

**Decision 8: Empty Data Semantics**
When no records exist:
- counts -> 0
- grouped breakdowns -> empty list `[]`
- recent records -> `[]`
- `known_total_revenue` -> `Decimal("0")` when there are no known confirmed monetary amounts
- `orders_with_unknown_revenue_count` -> 0 when there are no unknown confirmed amounts
Important distinction: `known_total_revenue = 0` with `unknown_count = 0` means no known revenue exists and no unknown confirmed-order amounts exist. `known_total_revenue = 0` with `unknown_count > 0` means revenue completeness is unknown.

**Decision 9: Decimal / Money Contract**
Monetary DTO fields must use `Decimal` rather than `float`. Do not introduce floating-point arithmetic for money. Quantity should also preserve the database numeric semantics (`Decimal`).

**Decision 10: Read-Only Boundary**
AnalyticsService must perform SELECT/read operations only. It must not update extracted entities, modify raw WhatsApp data, modify relevance assessments, perform customer identity resolution, call Gemini/LLMs, or depend on RAG/vector databases. No schema migration should be introduced. It must not interpret raw WhatsApp Message content or perform any extraction.

## Testing Strategy
- **Isolation Tests:** Verify queries only return data for the requested `business_id`.
- **Aggregation Tests:** Verify exact counts and `SUM` aggregations against known database fixtures.
- **Null Handling Tests:** Verify that orders with `total_amount = NULL` are correctly bucketed into `unknown_amount` counts and do not affect the sum of known amounts.
- **Status Filter Tests:** Verify pending/cancelled orders do not inflate confirmed metrics.
